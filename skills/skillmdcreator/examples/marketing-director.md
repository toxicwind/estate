# Mark Chen — Marketing Director

12 years in B2B SaaS marketing, currently leading a team of 15 across demand gen, content, product marketing, and brand at a Series C company ($40M ARR).

## Expertise areas

- **Brand strategy** — positioning, messaging frameworks (April Dunford approach), narrative
- **Demand generation** — paid search, paid social, account-based marketing, webinars
- **Content marketing** — SEO-driven blogs, thought leadership, video, podcasts
- **Marketing analytics** — attribution modelling, MQL/SQL/SAL pipelines, CAC, LTV, payback period
- **Team leadership** — hiring, coaching, performance management for 15 reports
- **Budget management** — currently running $2.4M annual marketing budget
- **Tools** — HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Analytics 4, Looker, Ahrefs, Clay, Default

## How I work

- **Strategy before tactics** — I won't greenlight campaigns without a clear hypothesis
- **Written arguments** — I ask for one-pagers before meetings; forces clear thinking
- **Review the pipeline, not the vanity** — impressions and engagement aren't the goal
- I manage by **outcomes not outputs** — what moved, not what shipped
- I'm **sceptical of attribution** — last-click is a lie, MTA is a model, not truth

## What I prioritise

- **Narrative and positioning** above all — if the story's wrong, the tactics don't matter
- **Qualified pipeline** — not leads, not MQLs, but pipeline that sales actually closes
- **Customer research** — I insist on 5 customer calls per quarter minimum
- **Team growth** — I'd rather have 3 great people than 6 average ones

## What I avoid

- Feature-led messaging — outcomes and problems first, always
- Random acts of marketing — every quarter has a clear theme
- Vanity metrics on slides I present to the CEO
- Agencies doing strategy work — they get execution, we own strategy

## Communication style

- Direct, numerate, short
- Written docs > decks for anything substantive
- I'll push back hard if something doesn't make sense — it's not personal
- I write my own first drafts and edit heavily; I don't trust AI-generated marketing copy unchanged

## Current focus

Rebuilding the positioning framework ahead of a Series D narrative — need to move from "tool" to "platform" positioning without losing the core audience.

## When helping me

- Challenge my thinking — tell me when an argument is weak
- Draft in a confident, direct voice — no hedging, no filler
- Show me the data behind claims; don't assert without backing
- I don't need marketing 101 — skip the introductions
- When drafting copy, keep sentences short and cut adjectives
