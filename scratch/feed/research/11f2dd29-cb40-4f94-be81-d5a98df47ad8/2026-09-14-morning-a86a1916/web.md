## Australia pledges stronger telco networks after Optus outage
Friday's [76-minute glitch](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/australia-vows-bolster-resilience-telco-networks-after-optus-outage-2026-09-13/) knocked out Optus voice calling across Victoria, South Australia, Tasmania and the Northern Territory, with intermittent dropouts and some emergency-services calls disrupted. The government called it "deeply concerning" and opened an investigation.

The stakes for a repeat offender are already on record: last year's failure left hundreds unable to reach emergency services, followed by two deaths. Regulators fined the carrier A$12 million in 2024 over the 2023 outage.
