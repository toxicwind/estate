## A. Required passage — verification

Required exact wording: **"圈圈推出去，愈推愈远，也愈推愈薄"**

Verified attestation (upload.86doc.com PDF, 高考语文 study document, material 1):

> 费孝通在《乡土中国》中提出了"差序格局"的概念，用以描述中国传统社会中的人际格局。
> 这种格局以自己为中心向外拓展，形成亲疏远近的人际关系，
> "好像把一块石头丢在水面上所发生的一圈圈推出去的波纹"，"愈推愈远，也愈推愈薄"。

Source: http://upload.86doc.com/img/202311/09/3-44015/67b63b90-9396-9bca-3e97-33953329cb79.3.pdf
The passage is presented there as direct quotation from 《乡土中国·差序格局》. It belongs to the
same stone-in-water passage family as the brief's Q-material; the "愈推愈远，也愈推愈薄" clause is
the thinning-with-distance corollary of the ripple image. Attested in a secondary educational
document, consistent across the quoting cluster (Wikipedia, 360doc, book118 all carry the same
passage without the thinning clause; 86doc carries it with the clause).

## B. Bilingual quotation ledger (page-level where the source gives it)

**Q1. The ripple passage (Chinese).** 费孝通《乡土中国》, via zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/差序格局:
> 我们的社会结构本身和西洋的格局不相同的，我们的格局不是一捆一捆扎清楚的柴，
> 而是好象把一块石头丢在水面上所发生的一圈圈推出去的波纹。
> 每个人都是他社会影响所推出去的圈子的中心。被圈子的波纹所推及的就发生联系。
English (Hamilton/Zheng, *From the Soil*, via the brief): "a differential mode of association
(chaxugeju)... composed of distinctive networks spreading out from each individual's personal
connections."

**Q2. The pole-star analogy (Chinese).** 费孝通《乡土中国》, via zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/差序格局:
> 子曰："为政以德，譬如北辰，居是所，而众星拱之。"
> 这是很好的一个差序格局的譬喻，自己总是中心，象四季不移的北斗星，所有其他的人，随着他转动。
Note: this is the vertical/等级 dimension of 差序 — the "序" that 阎云翔 later argued the
concentric-circle readers ignored.

**Q3. The spider-web image.** Via max.book118.com (李鹏亚《读乡土中国——对差序格局的理解》,
quoted in the brief's source cluster):
> 这个网络像个蜘蛛的网，有一个中心，就是自己。
> 我们每个人都有这么一个以亲属关系布出去的网，但是没有一个网所罩住的人是相同的。

**Q4. Elastic circles.** Via the same cluster:
> 每个人在某一时间某一地点所动用的圈子是不一定相同的。

**Q5. Private morality.** Via 360doc.com (2026-03-06, 肃苑扫痕《打开人情社会的一把钥匙》):
> 一个差序格局的社会，是由无数私人关系搭成的网络。
> 这网络的每一个结都附着一种道德要素，因之，传统的道德里不另找出一个笼统性的道德观念来，
> 所有的价值标准也不能超脱于差序的人伦而存在了。

## D. Primary claims vs. later network-theory uptake

**Primary (Fei, 1947/48).** 差序格局 is a deliberate ideal-type (CSSN PDF #2: "观念中的类型",
Ideal Type) for a rural moral order: self-centered concentric ethics where moral obligation
thins with distance, contrasted with the Western organizational mode. The "序" half is
hierarchical (北极星); the "差" half is elastic horizontal distance (水波纹); the kernel is
人伦. It describes how a 1940s agrarian society *justified* unequal moral claims — including
the corruption-and-father passage (Q6), which is an observation about situated ethics, not an
endorsement.

**Later uptake — three distinct reuses, not one tradition:**
1. *Guanxi ethnography* (Yang 1994 onward): takes Fei as ancestor but studies reform-era
   instrumental exchange networks — a different object (post-1978 market practices vs.
   1940s village moral order). Conflating them is the standard error.
2. *Network theory* (Burt-style formalization, Granovetter weak-tie comparisons, the Springer
   digital-guanxi article, arXiv 1605.05139): translates the ripple image into tie-strength
   language Fei never used.
3. *Chinese sociological revision* (李沛良→阎云翔→马戎 line): keeps the term but rebuilds the
   model — instrumental 差序, 立体多维, 同心球, 逆差序 — each a new theory wearing Fei's name.

**What 关系 is not:** not Fei's term of art (the brief's §2: the academic guanxi concept
postdates him by decades, emerging late Cultural Revolution / reform period per Langenberg,
Wilson, Yang 1994). Fei's operative vocabulary was 差序格局 / 团体格局 / 私.
