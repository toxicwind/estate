## The 横 / 纵 (horizontal / vertical) decomposition

Paper 2 gives the cleanest analytical split (p. 89–90):

1. **水波纹 (water ripples)** — the horizontal dimension: "推己及人" 的人际
   交往模式, kinship and locality as the key bonds, the relativity of
   公私 and 群己 relations. This is the 差 (differential distance) axis.
2. **北极星 (North Star)** — the vertical dimension: "作为社会格局的结构",
   the continuous reproduction of hierarchical difference through social and
   cultural institutions. This is the 序 (ordering) axis.
3. **人伦 (human relations / Confucian role ethics)** — the basic kernel:
   儒家伦理 as the role norms between individuals that shape social order.

"差序格局"穿越历史时空的普适性学术魅力在于它对社会结构的横、纵两个维度
的同时把握 — the concept's lasting power is its simultaneous grasp of both
dimensions.

## 差序格局 vs 关系 — separation maintained

Neither paper treats 关系 (guanxi) as 费孝通's own term of art:

- Paper 1 uses 关系 in the ordinary sense (人际关系, 社会关系, 血缘关系,
  地缘关系) — relationship as a general noun, not the guanxi-studies concept.
- Paper 2's framework is 费孝通's 1947/48 model plus its later academic
  reception (描述/建构/理解三路径). The modern guanxi literature
  (post-1980s ethnography, network theory) is a separate downstream
  adaptation, not present in 费孝通's own vocabulary.
- Paper 2 explicitly notes the model is value-neutral as an ideal type
  ("和价值判断没有任何关系") and that 费孝通 himself admitted other systems
  also shaped Chinese society ("并不排斥其他体系同样影响着中国社会").
