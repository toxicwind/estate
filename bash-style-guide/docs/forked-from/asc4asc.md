# asc4asc/bash-style-guide

Source: https://github.com/asc4asc/bash-style-guide

## Rules adopted

- 2-space indent (fork of bahamas10 that changed tabs to spaces)

## Quoted text from upstream

> Spaces (2) because I often change the editor and the system.
> Columns not to exceed 80.
> No more than 1 blank line in a row.

## Notes

asc4asc is a working fork of bahamas10. The estate tracks asc4asc for
indentation because the change is deliberate and documented.
